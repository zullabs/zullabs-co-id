Bahasa IndonesiaEnglish

Siapa kami

Zul Labs Agen dibuat dan dikelola oleh PT Zul Labs Teknologi, Sibolga, Indonesia. Pertanyaan tentang privasi: admin@zullabs.co.id.

Apa yang dilakukan Zul Labs Agen

Zul Labs Agen menyambungkan browser Anda (ekstensi di komputer atau browser di dalam aplikasi Android) dengan asisten AI Zul Labs di WhatsApp. Saat Anda memberi perintah di WhatsApp, asisten mengirim langkah-langkah (buka, baca, klik, ketik, gulir, tangkap layar, unggah) ke perangkat Anda, dan perangkat Anda mengirim hasilnya kembali. Ekstensi hanya bekerja di satu tab khusus. Aplikasi Android hanya bekerja di browser di dalam aplikasinya sendiri.

Data yang tidak kami ambil

  • Kata sandi. Kolom kata sandi tidak pernah dibaca atau diisi asisten; Anda selalu mengetiknya sendiri.
  • Tab lain di browser Anda, riwayat browser Anda, cookie, atau aplikasi lain di HP Anda.
  • Lokasi, kontak, SMS, mikrofon, atau kamera.
  • Berkas di perangkat Anda. Berkas hanya dipakai bila Anda sendiri mengirimnya di WhatsApp dan meminta asisten mengunggahnya ke sebuah situs; mengunggah selalu menunggu izin Anda.

Ke mana data pergi

Data dikirim terenkripsi (HTTPS) melalui Google Firebase (Authentication dan Cloud Firestore) ke server asisten Zul Labs, lalu ke penyedia model AI yang menjalankan asisten (saat ini Google Gemini) agar perintah Anda bisa diproses. Penyedia ini bertindak sebagai pemroses data untuk kami. Kami tidak menjual data Anda, tidak memakainya untuk iklan, dan tidak membagikannya kepada pihak lain di luar keperluan menjalankan layanan.

Berapa lama data disimpan

  • Perintah dan hasilnya (termasuk tangkapan layar) dihapus otomatis paling lama 7 hari setelah dibuat.
  • Kode sambung berlaku 10 menit dan dihapus setelah dipakai.
  • Data perangkat yang tersambung disimpan sampai Anda memutuskannya.
  • Catatan aksi di jendela ekstensi atau aplikasi disimpan di perangkat Anda sendiri (100 entri terakhir).

Kendali Anda

  • Aksi penting (kirim, posting, bayar, beli, hapus, ikuti, unggah) selalu menunggu izin Anda. Tidak dijawab dalam 2 menit berarti ditolak.
  • Tombol STOP menghentikan asisten seketika.
  • Putuskan perangkat kapan saja dengan /putus di WhatsApp, tombol Putuskan di ekstensi, atau menu ⋮ di aplikasi.
  • Untuk meminta salinan atau penghapusan data Anda, kirim email ke admin@zullabs.co.id dari atau dengan menyebutkan nomor WhatsApp Anda. Kami memprosesnya paling lama 14 hari.

Anak-anak

Zul Labs Agen ditujukan untuk pengguna berusia 18 tahun ke atas dan tidak ditujukan untuk anak-anak.

Perubahan

Bila kebijakan ini berubah, tanggal di bagian atas diperbarui. Perubahan penting juga kami sampaikan lewat asisten WhatsApp.

Zul Labs Agen Privacy Policy

Applies to the browser extension "Zul Labs Agen — Browser" and the Android app "ZL Agen" (id.zullabs.agen). Last updated 26 November 2026.

Who we are

Zul Labs Agen is made and operated by PT Zul Labs Teknologi, Sibolga, Indonesia. Privacy questions: admin@zullabs.co.id.

What Zul Labs Agen does

Zul Labs Agen connects your browser (the extension on a computer, or the browser inside the Android app) to the Zul Labs AI assistant on WhatsApp. When you give a request on WhatsApp, the assistant sends steps (open, read, click, type, scroll, screenshot, upload) to your device, and your device sends the results back. The extension works in one dedicated tab only. The Android app works only in its own built-in browser.

Data we do not take

  • Passwords. Password fields are never read or filled by the assistant; you always type them yourself.
  • Other browser tabs, your browsing history, cookies, or other apps on your phone.
  • Location, contacts, SMS, microphone or camera.
  • Files on your device. A file is only used when you send it on WhatsApp yourself and ask the assistant to upload it to a site; uploading always waits for your approval.

Where data goes

Data is sent encrypted (HTTPS) through Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore) to the Zul Labs assistant server, and then to the AI model provider that runs the assistant (currently Google Gemini) so your request can be processed. These providers act as processors on our behalf. We do not sell your data, do not use it for advertising, and do not share it with anyone beyond what is needed to run the service.

How long data is kept

  • Commands and their results (including screenshots) are deleted automatically within 7 days of creation.
  • Pairing codes are valid for 10 minutes and deleted once used.
  • Connected-device records are kept until you disconnect.
  • The action log in the extension or app window is stored on your own device (last 100 entries).

Your controls

  • Important actions (send, post, pay, buy, delete, follow, upload) always wait for your approval. No answer within 2 minutes means no.
  • The STOP button halts the assistant immediately.
  • Disconnect a device at any time with /putus on WhatsApp, the Disconnect button in the extension, or the ⋮ menu in the app.
  • To request a copy or deletion of your data, email admin@zullabs.co.id from, or quoting, your WhatsApp number. We handle requests within 14 days.

Children

Zul Labs Agen is intended for users aged 18 and over and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top is updated. Significant changes are also announced through the WhatsApp assistant.

Zul Labs Agen's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Chrome Web Store and Google Play User Data Policies, including the Limited Use requirements.
